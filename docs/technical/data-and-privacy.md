# Data and privacy

Brightdesk is designed for local use. Customer work stays on the device rather than being sent to a Brightdesk cloud workspace.

## Desktop product boundary

- No customer account, online sign-in or password is required.
- Product work is stored locally on the customer's device.
- Cloud synchronisation and live multi-user collaboration are not part of the approved product scope.
- Brightdesk does not include advertising, customer analytics, behavioural tracking or telemetry.
- Customer documents and other work are not uploaded to an external AI service by Brightdesk.

Individual product references describe important data behaviours, including local form responses, lists, plans, tasks and workspaces.

## Help website boundary

Brightdesk Help and Technical Documentation are separate static, read-only websites.

- Visitors do not create accounts or sign in.
- The sites do not contain contact forms, comments, uploads or public editing.
- Search runs in the browser against the local page index. Search terms are not sent to an external service or stored as visitor profiles.
- The sites do not use advertising, analytics, tracking scripts, external fonts, external scripts or external images.

## What this means

Readers can consult the Help and technical reference pages without connecting their desktop work to the website. Opening a documentation link never launches or controls the installed Brightdesk products.
