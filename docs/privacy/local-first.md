# Local-first: what it means

Brightdesk is designed around work kept on your device.

That statement is a product direction, not a substitute for explaining each feature. Some functions, such as checking for product updates or retrieving approved language resources, may need an internet connection. Their exact behaviour must be documented separately and verified against the implementation.

## What this page does not claim

- It does not claim every feature works without an internet connection.
- It does not claim that every operating system has passed offline testing.
- It does not describe a cloud backup or synchronisation service.
- It does not replace the privacy information shipped with a verified release.

When a guide explains a feature that uses the internet, it must say what the connection is for and what data is exchanged.
