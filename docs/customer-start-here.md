# Get started with Brightdesk Suite

Brightdesk Suite is the starting point for the ten approved Brightdesk desktop products. Use it to choose the products you want in your Suite and open each product in its own window.

Brightdesk is free forever. Brightdesk Suite, all ten approved products and this customer help centre are free for every customer. There are no subscriptions, paid tiers, trials, licence fees or charges to read the Wiki. Customers do not need an account, sign-in or password. Your work is kept on your device.

**Desktop-only support:** Brightdesk Suite and all ten products run on desktop computers using Windows, macOS or Linux. There is no mobile application or mobile version for phones or tablets.

## Before you start

For the focused walkthrough, see [Install and update Brightdesk](everyday/install-update.html). The steps below remain the Suite getting-started summary.

1. Use the official Brightdesk installer for your computer when a verified release is available.
2. Open the installer and follow the instructions shown by your operating system.
3. You do not need to install Node.js or use Terminal or Command Prompt to run Brightdesk.
4. Read the Brightdesk Terms and Conditions when they appear.

Choose **Accept** or **Agree** to continue. Choosing **Decline** or **Disagree**, or closing the required agreement without accepting, prevents the product workspace from opening.

Only use release installers that have passed the checks for your operating system. A configured installer format does not by itself mean that a verified download is available.

### Repair and recovery

A repair procedure has not yet been verified for the current Brightdesk Suite release, so this guide does not invent repair buttons or steps. Do not choose **Remove** or delete local project files as a repair attempt. Use only repair instructions supplied with an official Brightdesk release after that procedure has been checked for your operating system.

## Use Home

When Brightdesk Suite opens, **Home** shows the product screen. The top bar reads **Your apps**, and the product grid is headed **Your products**.

Each card shows whether that product is already **In your Suite** or **Available to add**. The ten products are:

| Product | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| [Pages](products/pages.html) | Letters, reports and formatted documents |
| [Sheets](products/sheets.html) | Spreadsheet data, tables and calculations |
| [Decks](products/decks.html) | Slide presentations |
| [Notes](products/notes.html) | Quick notes and ideas |
| [Database](products/database.html) | Local tables, fields and records |
| [Forms](products/forms.html) | Forms and responses stored on this device |
| [Lists](products/lists.html) | Structured lists and records |
| [Planner](products/planner.html) | Plans, buckets and tasks |
| [To Do](products/to-do.html) | Personal tasks and due dates |
| [Workspace](products/workspace.html) | Project pages, notes and checklists |

Choose a product guide above for help with that product's features.

## Add or remove products

1. Select **Apps** in the left navigation.
2. Find the product you want.
3. Choose **Add to Suite** to make an available product part of your Suite.
4. Choose **Remove from Suite** to remove a product workspace from the Suite.

Adding a product makes its workspace available inside Brightdesk Suite. It does not install a separate desktop product package.

Removing a product from the Suite also does not uninstall a separately installed product and does not delete your local documents or project data.

## Open a product

1. Return to **Home**.
2. Find a product marked as being in your Suite.
3. Choose **Open** on its card.

The product opens in its own window. Brightdesk Suite stays open.

Each product has its own window. Choosing **Open** again brings that product's existing window to the front. Closing one product window leaves the Suite and your other product windows open.

When you are working inside a product window, use **Switch product** to move to another product that is included and visible in your Suite.

## Switch between products

The Suite keeps its Home window open while each product opens in its own window.

### Open another product from the Suite

1. Return to the Suite window.
2. Choose **Home** or **Apps** in the left navigation.
3. Find a product card marked **In your Suite**.
4. Choose **Open** on that card.
5. The product opens in its own window. The Suite window stays open.

Choosing **Open** again brings that product's existing window to the front. It does not create another copy of the same product window.

### Switch products from a product window

1. Look in the left navigation of the product window for **Switch product**.
2. Choose another product shown in that list.
3. Brightdesk opens that product window, or brings its existing window to the front.

The list shows products that are included in the Suite and have not been hidden from the list. A product that is not listed may not be included in the Suite, or it may have been hidden.

### Show or hide a product in the Switch product list

1. Return to the Suite window.
2. Choose **Apps** in the left navigation.
3. Find the product card marked **In your Suite**.
4. Open the card's three-dot **Product settings** menu.
5. Choose **Hide from Switch product** to remove that product from the navigation list.

To show it again, open the same product card menu and choose **Show in Switch product**.

Hiding a product changes its visibility in the **Switch product** list only. It does not remove the product from the Suite. It does not uninstall a separate desktop product. It does not delete your documents or project data.

### Add or remove a product from the Suite

- To add a product, choose **Apps**, find a card marked **Available to add**, then choose **Add to Suite**.
- To remove a product from the Suite, find a card marked **In your Suite** and choose **Remove from Suite**. Read the confirmation message before continuing.

Removing a product changes which bundled workspaces are available inside the Suite. It does not uninstall a separately installed desktop product. It does not delete your documents or project data. Add the product to the Suite again when you need that workspace there.

## Use Files

Choose **Files** in the left navigation to access the Suite's local file actions.

The Files page provides **Open file** and **Save project** actions. It is not a full file browser or a list of recent files. Each product also has its own file controls where supported.

Use only file types offered by the current product. Keep a Brightdesk project copy when you plan to continue editing your work.

### Open an existing file

1. Choose **Files** in the left navigation.
2. Under **Open a file**, choose **Open file**.
3. Find the file on your computer and select it.
4. Brightdesk opens the file in the matching product workspace.

The current open-file types are:

| File type | Opens in |
| --- | --- |
| `.bdesk` | A Brightdesk project |
| `.docx` | Pages |
| `.xlsx`, `.xlsm`, `.csv`, `.tsv` | Sheets |
| `.pptx` | Decks |
| `.txt` | Notes |
| `.md`, `.html` | Pages |

A file must be in a supported format. Changing a file's name or extension does not convert it to another format.

### Save a Brightdesk project

1. Choose **Files** in the left navigation.
2. Under **Save your project**, choose **Save project**.
3. Choose where on your computer to save it.
4. Enter a file name and save it.

Brightdesk saves a project file with the `.bdesk` extension. It keeps the Brightdesk project data together so you can open the project again later. It is different from exporting a separate Word document, spreadsheet, presentation, PDF or text copy.

### Export a copy from a product

When you are working in Pages, Sheets, Decks or Notes, use that product's **Export** control and choose an available format:

| Product | Available export formats |
| --- | --- |
| Pages | DOCX or HTML; PDF is available from **Home → PDF** |
| Sheets | XLSX or CSV |
| Decks | PPTX |
| Notes | Markdown or plain text |

The export is a separate copy. Keep a Brightdesk project file as well when you want to reopen your work in Brightdesk. Other products have their own save or export instructions in their product guide.

## Notifications, Settings and languages

For the full step-by-step language walkthrough, see [Languages and spelling](everyday/languages-and-spelling.html).

- Select the **Notifications** bell to view notification history. Choose **Clear all** only when you intend to clear that history.
- Select the **Settings** cogwheel in the top-right corner.
- Choose **Languages & dictionaries** to open the language controls.
- Under **Display language**, choose an installed language to change Brightdesk menus, labels and messages. The change applies straight away.
- To add another display language, find it under **Available language packs** and choose **Install**. When it appears under **Installed language packs**, you can select it as the display language.
- Under **Proofing language**, choose the language you want to use for spelling checks. This is separate from the display language.
- Under **Available spelling dictionaries**, search for a language or region. Choose **Install** on the dictionary you need.
- Under **Check the current document**, choose an installed dictionary and select **Check spelling**.
- Read the results. The check reports possible spelling issues and suggestions; it does not change your document.
- Use **Back to apps** when you have finished.

The spelling status can vary by operating system and installed resources. Read the status shown beside **Proofing language** rather than assuming that every language is supported on every computer. Spelling checks apply to the open Pages, Sheets, Decks or Notes document.

The notification area can show official Brightdesk update notices when they are available. However, the current Suite does not yet have a configured update-publishing provider, so live update delivery is **not active or verified**. Do not expect an update download to be available until an approved Brightdesk release is actually published. Downloading and installing, when available, require a deliberate action.

## Troubleshooting and common questions

**Why can't I open a file?** Check that it is one of the supported file types listed above. A file extension change does not convert the file. If the file type is not supported, use the original programme to save or export a supported format.

**Why does an imported file look different?** Some advanced formatting or content may not transfer exactly. Check the result after opening it, and keep your original file until you are happy with the imported copy.

**Where is my saved Brightdesk project?** It is saved in the folder you selected when you chose **Files → Save project**. Look for the `.bdesk` file name you chose.

**Why is a spelling dictionary not available?** Check that the dictionary appears under **Installed spelling dictionaries**. Search the available catalogue and install it first. The proofing status also tells you when spelling support is unavailable for the selected language or computer.

**Can I receive a Brightdesk update now?** The current Suite does not yet have a configured update-publishing provider. Update delivery is not active or verified, so a successful setup of the update controls should not be assumed.

## Try this example: start a small project

1. Open **Apps** and add **Pages** and **Planner** to your Suite if they are not already included.
2. Return to **Home** and open **Planner**.
3. Create a plan for your project and add two or three tasks.
4. Open **Pages** from the Suite and write a separate project brief.
5. Save the work in each product using its own **Save project** action where available.

**What you should have:** a project task board in Planner and a separate written brief in Pages. The Suite opens the products together, but the files stay separate; it does not automatically combine or synchronise the documents.
## Keep your work local

Brightdesk products are designed to work on your device. They do not require a customer account or cloud storage to open and use local work.

Use the language controls to choose a display language or proofing language where supported. Language resources are supplied through official Brightdesk language updates. Do not install dictionary files from unapproved sources.

## Understand the help guide labels

| Label | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Verified** | The steps were run in the named product version and operating system. |
| **Source-confirmed** | The current implementation or tests support the feature, but the complete user workflow has not been tested. |
| **Needs review** | The page has not yet been checked against a complete workflow in a current desktop build. |
| **Under test** | Work exists, but testing is not complete. |
| **Planned** | The feature is intended for later and is not available guidance. |
| **Not available** | The current product scope does not include it. |

These guides must not describe a feature as verified until it has been tested in the running desktop product. Interface design artwork is not a genuine product screenshot.
