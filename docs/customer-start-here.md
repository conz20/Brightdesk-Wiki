# Get started with Brightdesk Suite

Brightdesk Suite is the starting point for the ten approved Brightdesk desktop products. Use it to choose the products you want in your Suite and open each product in its own window.

Brightdesk is free forever. There are no subscriptions, paid tiers, customer accounts, sign-ins or passwords. The products are designed for local use, and your work is kept on your device.

## Before you start

1. Use the official Brightdesk installer for your computer when a verified release is available.
2. Open the installer and follow the instructions shown by your operating system.
3. You do not need to install Node.js or use Terminal or Command Prompt to run Brightdesk.
4. Read the Brightdesk Terms and Conditions when they appear.

Choose **Accept** or **Agree** to continue. Choosing **Decline** or **Disagree**, or closing the required agreement without accepting, prevents the product workspace from opening.

Only use release installers that have passed the checks for your operating system. A configured installer format does not by itself mean that a verified download is available.

## Use Home

When Brightdesk Suite opens, Home shows the ten product cards. The page is headed **Your products**.

Each card shows whether that product is already in your Suite or is available to add. The ten products are:

| --- | --- |
| [Pages](products/pages.html) | Letters, reports and formatted documents |
| [Sheets](products/sheets.html) | Spreadsheet data, tables and calculations |
| [Decks](products/decks.html) | Slide presentations |
| [Notes](products/notes.html) | Quick notes and ideas |
| [Database](products/database.html) | Local tables, fields and records |
| [Forms](products/forms.html) | Forms and responses stored on this device |
| [Lists](products/lists.html) | Structured lists and records |

Choose a product guide above for help with that product's features.

## Add or remove products

1. Select **Apps** in the left navigation.
2. Find the product you want.
3. Choose **Add to Suite** to make an available product part of your Suite.
4. Choose **Remove from Suite** to remove a product workspace from the Suite.

Adding a product makes its workspace available inside Brightdesk Suite. It does not install a separate desktop product package.

Removing a product from the Suite also does not uninstall a separately installed product and does not delete your local documents or project data.

## Open a product

1. Return to **Home**.
2. Find a product marked as being in your Suite.
3. Choose **Open** on its card.

The product opens in its own window. Brightdesk Suite stays open.

Each product has its own window. Choosing **Open** again brings that product's existing window to the front. Closing one product window leaves the Suite and your other product windows open.

When you are working inside a product window, use **Switch product** to move to another product that is included and visible in your Suite.

## Use Files

Choose **Files** in the left navigation to access the Suite's local file actions.

The Files page provides **Open file** and **Save project** actions. It is not a full file browser or a list of recent files. Each product also has its own file controls where supported.

Use only file types offered by the current product. Keep a Brightdesk project copy when you plan to continue editing your work.

## Notifications and Settings

- Select the **Notifications** bell to view desktop notifications.
- Select the **Settings** cogwheel to open Brightdesk Settings.

Update notifications and downloads are shown only when an official Brightdesk update is available. Downloading and installing an update require a deliberate action.

## Keep your work local

Brightdesk products are designed to work on your device. They do not require a customer account or cloud storage to open and use local work.

Use the language controls to choose a display language or proofing language where supported. Language resources are supplied through official Brightdesk language updates. Do not install dictionary files from unapproved sources.

## Understand the help guide labels

| Label | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Verified** | The steps were run in the named product version and operating system. |
| **Source-confirmed** | The current implementation or tests support the feature, but the complete user workflow has not been tested. |
| **Needs review** | The page has not yet been checked against a complete workflow in a current desktop build. |
| **Under test** | Work exists, but testing is not complete. |
| **Planned** | The feature is intended for later and is not available guidance. |
| **Not available** | The current product scope does not include it. |

These guides must not describe a feature as verified until it has been tested in the running desktop product. Interface design artwork is not a genuine product screenshot.
